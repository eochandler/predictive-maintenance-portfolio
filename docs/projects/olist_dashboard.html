Where Olist's marketplace loses customers — and where it doesn't

An analysis of 96,478 delivered orders across Brazil (2016–2018), built to answer one operational question: is late delivery actually costing us customer satisfaction, and where should logistics investment go first?

Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset Real, anonymized marketplace data · 99K orders · 3K sellers · SQL analysis via DuckDB

The core finding

Delivery timeliness isn't a minor operational detail — it's the single strongest driver of review score in this dataset.

A late delivery drops the average review score by 1.73 points — nearly two full stars — affecting roughly 8.1% of all delivered orders in this dataset. Recommendation: prioritize carrier/logistics investment in the states with both high order volume and high average delivery time (see State Performance below) before optimizing anywhere else.

Revenue & late-delivery rate over time

Revenue grew steadily month over month. Late-delivery rate is noisier — with a sharp spike around Nov 2017 and again in early 2018, both worth investigating against known logistics events (e.g. holiday volume, carrier disruptions).

Category risk: revenue vs. late-delivery rate

Categories in the upper right generate high revenue but also carry the highest late-delivery risk — these are the highest-value logistics-improvement targets.

State performance

Delivery time varies enormously by state — from 8.7 days in São Paulo (the hub) to over 24 days in more remote states — and review scores track this gap closely.

Payment methods

Credit card dominates, with installment plans averaging 3.5 payments — common in the Brazilian market.

Methodology

All figures computed via SQL (DuckDB) directly against the raw Olist CSVs — no spreadsheet pre-aggregation. Orders with status other than "delivered" are excluded from revenue and review figures. Months with fewer than 100 orders (partial data at the start/end of the collection period) are excluded from trend charts. "Late" is defined as delivered after the carrier's own estimated delivery date.
