Predictive Maintenance, Business Intelligence & Experimentation

Executive Summary

Welcome to my portfolio! I build end-to-end data science solutions across two areas: industrial predictive maintenance (failure forecasting, real-time telemetry monitoring, and cost-optimized maintenance decisions) and general business analytics (SQL-driven BI dashboards, customer churn modeling, and rigorous A/B testing).

This portfolio demonstrates a complete analytics lifecycle across six projects:

Predictive Maintenance

  1. Failure Prediction (ML Pipeline): Forecasting Remaining Useful Life (RUL) from multi-sensor time-series telemetry, validated on a held-out test set.
  2. Operational Telemetry (Streaming UI): Building real-time interactive dashboards with dynamic anomaly alerting.
  3. Business Impact (Decision Engine): Translating real model predictions into optimal financial policies that minimize total operational expenditure (OPEX).

Business Analytics

  1. Marketplace BI Dashboard: Answering a real stakeholder question with SQL, run directly against 96K e-commerce orders — where is the business losing revenue and customer satisfaction in the delivery pipeline.
  2. Customer Churn Prediction: Comparing model architectures and building a per-customer cost-optimized retention threshold, saving $283K over a do-nothing baseline on 7,043 real customers.
  3. A/B Testing & Experimentation: A full-rigor hypothesis test on 294K real users — data contamination handling, sample-ratio-mismatch checks, confidence intervals, and statistical power analysis.

See the Methodology & Limitations page for how the predictive maintenance projects connect and where their current constraints are.
